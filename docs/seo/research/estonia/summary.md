10. **Verdict:** in Estonia the money is in "kodulehe/e-poe tegemine" (CPC €8–17). App/MVP/CTO positioning has almost no local search demand. Treat Estonia as a website/WordPress + e-shop market, or skip local SEO for the app offer.

**Method.** Volume and CPC (USD) come from Google Ads via yepapi `/v1/seo/keywords` (location 2233) where available, otherwise from Labs. Suggestions come from Labs ideas/related (ET only; Labs has no Russian-language data for Estonia). RU expansions are hand-built variants, each volume-checked. KD comes from Labs (`/keywords/difficulty`); it's blank where Labs had none. Dropped: volume-0 terms unless they were a seed or an API-suggested commercial term; also banking/eID apps, jobs, internships, private-person names. "10" is the Ads floor bucket. Language-neutral Latin terms (woocommerce, flutter, MVP) are listed under ET only.
