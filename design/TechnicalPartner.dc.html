What is a fractional CTO?

A fractional CTO is an experienced technical leader who works for your company part-time, usually a set number of days per month. You get the decisions and oversight of a CTO while your product and team are still too small for a full-time hire.

With me that also means hands-on help: I can review a pull request, fix a store rejection or prototype a feature myself when that is faster than explaining it.
